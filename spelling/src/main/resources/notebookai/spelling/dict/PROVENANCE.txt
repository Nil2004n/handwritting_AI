PROVENANCE of the bundled English word lists (notebook-ai spelling assistance)

What they are
  SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), version 2020.12.07, by Kevin Atkinson, upstream
  https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist  (project page http://wordlist.sourceforge.net/), in the plain, already-expanded word-list form that
  Debian builds from the source package "scowl" 2020.12.07-4. They are NOT Microsoft, Office or Windows dictionaries and nothing here is
  Microsoft-backed or Microsoft-equivalent.

Files (bytes exactly as shipped in the Debian packages; one entry per line, UTF-8, NFC, no duplicates; checked at load time against SHA256SUMS)
  en-US.txt         american-english         (Debian package wamerican        2020.12.07-4)  full list   (~104k entries)
  en-US.common.txt  american-english-small   (Debian package wamerican-small  2020.12.07-4)  smaller list of the more common words (~51k)
  en-GB.txt         british-english          (wbritish        2020.12.07-4)
  en-GB.common.txt  british-english-small    (wbritish-small  2020.12.07-4)
  en-CA.txt         canadian-english         (wcanadian       2020.12.07-4)
  en-CA.common.txt  canadian-english-small   (wcanadian-small 2020.12.07-4)
  The "common" list is used ONLY as a commonness tier when ranking suggestions. It is not word-frequency data and not a calibrated score.
  The lists contain proper nouns, possessives ('s forms), contractions and accented words; they are a spelling list, not a vocabulary of "correct" text.
